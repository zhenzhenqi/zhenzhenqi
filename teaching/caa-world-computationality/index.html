Sep – Oct, 2025 · 汪洋 WANG Yang

写在前面

我们的世界被层层的技术调解，技术对生活的影响如此巨大它几乎要获得主体性。本课程将创作者（不管是艺术家还是设计师或者其他文化工作者）重新定位为一股重要的再定向力量 — 一定程度上获得一些对于技术的清醒以及转动方向盘的实在力。技术并非中立的工具；它是一种拥有自身历史、政治和社会影响力的强大媒介。为了让创作者承担起这一责任，创作者们必须首先理解技术的根本性质及其对我们的深远影响。

本课程分为两个部分。首先，我们将通过探究丰富的技术思想史来建立批判性的基础。我们将重温技术哲学和软件研究中的部分核心思想，超越简单的叙述，将技术视为我们人类体验中构成性的一部分，而非一系列不相关的工具。

其次，我们将通过"打开黑箱"的方式来回应当前的人工智能潮。通过使用TensorFlow和Keras等框架的实践方法，学生将从零开始构建一个非常基础的神经网络模型，揭示这些系统底层逻辑的工作原理。尽管这简单的模型与当今最大的模型相比显得原始，但其基本原理是相同的，这为艺术家提供了至关重要的清晰认知。

通过将批判性论述与动手实践相结合，本课程赋予艺术家一个全新的视角——使他们不仅能够认识自己在历史思想洪流中的位置，还能理解周遭变化的速率。有了这些知识，他们可以重塑自己与技术的关系，成为历史方向的主动塑造者，而非被动的乘客。

Our world is mediated layer upon layer by technology. The influence of technology on life is so immense that it almost acquires subjectivity of its own. This course repositions creators—whether artists, designers, or other cultural workers—as a vital re-orienting force, endowing them with a measure of lucidity about technology and a tangible capacity to grasp the steering wheel. Technology is not a neutral tool; it is a powerful medium with its own history, politics, and social influence. For creators to shoulder this responsibility, they must first understand the fundamental nature of technology and its far-reaching impact on us.

The course unfolds in two parts. First, we will build a critical foundation by delving into the rich intellectual history of technology. We will revisit key ideas in philosophy of technology and software studies, treating technology as a constitutive element of human experience rather than a collection of isolated tools.

Second, we will respond to the current wave of artificial intelligence by “opening the black box.” Through hands-on work with frameworks like TensorFlow and Keras, students will construct a very basic neural network model from scratch. This exercise exposes the underlying logic of these systems. While such a simple model may appear primitive compared to today’s largest systems, the core principles are the same—offering artists crucial clarity.

By combining critical discourse with practical making, the course provides artists with a fresh perspective: they can situate themselves within the flow of historical thought while also grasping the speed of the changes around them. With this knowledge, they can reshape their relationship to technology and become active shapers of history’s direction, rather than passive passengers.
